## How to Download Safely

### Step 1: Choose Image
1. Go to one of the recommended websites
2. Search for "happy customers" or "satisfied clients"
3. Choose image that matches your brand

### Step 2: Check License
1. **Look for license information** on the image page
2. **Verify commercial use is allowed**
3. **Check if attribution is required**

### Step 3: Download
1. **Click download button**
2. **Choose appropriate size** (800x600 for web)
3. **Save to public/images/ folder**
